Shots and danger are a two-sided blend: the opponent’s shot generation against your goalie’s team suppression, then a league-constant venue factor (home ×0.985 shots, ×0.990 danger). Danger is expected goals per shot plus half the shrunk finishing talent. Save percentage adds the goalie’s own goals-saved-above-expected rate, shrunk with a 6,000-shot prior. Team inputs are MoneyPuck 2023-24 through 2025-26, last season weighted half. No rest or back-to-back term.
